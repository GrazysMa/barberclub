What we collect

  • Booking details: name, phone number, email, note for the barber, chosen service, barber, date and time, booking code.
  • Visit history: completed, cancelled and missed visits, the barber’s notes about the service.
  • Ratings: stars and a comment, if you leave them after a visit.
  • Data from our previous booking system: if you booked with us through Treatwell before, we exported the salon’s client list from Treatwell and transferred your first and last name, phone, email, number of visits, the date your client record was created and whether you had agreed to receive news in Treatwell. We use this only to serve your visits; news is sent only to those who agreed.

Why we use it and on what basis

  • To book and serve your visit – confirmation, reminder, contacting you about changes. Basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To ask you to rate your visit and improve our service. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can opt out at any time.
  • News and offers by email – only if you agreed. Basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • The client list transferred from Treatwell – so we can keep serving you after changing our booking system. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object and ask us to delete your data.

Who sees your data

Salon staff: a barber sees their own clients, the salon managers see all. These service providers (processors) store and deliver the data for us:

  • Supabase – database, servers in the European Union.
  • Resend – sending booking confirmation and reminder emails (EU region). This feature is still being set up: until it is switched on, we do not send emails to clients.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare – website hosting and domain; they see technical data such as your IP address.

Where a provider is outside the EU, data is transferred under the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Public reviews

If you leave a comment, the website shows it with your first name (if you also entered a surname when booking, only its first letter), the barber, the service and the date. Your phone and email are never shown. The salon can hide a comment, and you can ask us to delete it.

How long we keep it

Booking and client card data: no longer than 3 years after your last visit (for clients transferred from Treatwell, counted from the transfer in October 2026 if you have not visited since). We review and delete old records at least once a year. Ratings: until you ask us to delete them or this system is closed. Consent for news: until you withdraw it.

Cookies

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. Visits are counted without cookies (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Your browser only stores your chosen language, and the login for staff.

Do you have to provide your data

To book online, your name, phone number and email are required – without them we cannot book your visit. A note for the barber and a rating are optional. If you prefer not to give your email, you can book by phone.

Automated decisions

We make no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you and do no profiling.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format and withdraw consent. Call +370 603 18712, email indrearslan@gmail.com or ask at the salon. We reply within 30 days.

If you think your data is handled unlawfully, you can complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt).
