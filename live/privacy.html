Kas mato jūsų duomenis

Kai paslaugų teikėjas yra už ES ribų, duomenys perduodami taikant Europos Komisijos patvirtintas standartines sutarčių sąlygas. Duomenų neparduodame ir neperduodame reklamai.

Privacy policy

Updated: 2026-10-18

Who handles your data

The data controller is Barber Club Vilnius, K. Ulvydo g. 11-432, Vilnius, 08355, Lithuania. Phone +370 603 18712.

What we collect

  • Booking details: name, phone number, email, note for the barber, chosen service, barber, date and time, booking code.
  • Visit history: completed, cancelled and missed visits, the barber’s notes about the service.
  • Ratings: stars and a comment, if you leave them after a visit.
  • Data from our previous booking system: if you booked through Treatwell before, we transferred your name, phone, email, number of visits and whether you agreed to receive news.

Why we use it and on what basis

  • To book and serve your visit – confirmation, reminder, contacting you about changes. Basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To ask you to rate your visit and improve our service. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can opt out at any time.
  • News and offers by email – only if you agreed. Basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.

Who sees your data

Salon staff: a barber sees their own clients, the salon managers see all. These service providers (processors) store and deliver the data for us:

  • Supabase – database, servers in the European Union.
  • Resend – sending visit emails (EU region).
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare – website hosting and domain; they see technical data such as your IP address.

Where a provider is outside the EU, data is transferred under the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Public reviews

If you leave a comment, the website shows it with your first name and the first letter of your surname, the barber and the date. Your phone and email are never shown.

How long we keep it

Booking and client card data: 3 years after your last visit, then deleted. Ratings: until you delete them or this system is closed. Consent for news: until you withdraw it.

Cookies

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. Visits are counted without cookies (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Your browser only stores your chosen language, and the login for staff.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format and withdraw consent. Call +370 603 18712 or ask at the salon. We reply within 30 days.

If you think your data is handled unlawfully, you can complain to the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt).
